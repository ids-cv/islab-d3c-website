abstract: We performed machine learning using spectrum analysis and power spectra
  of electroencephalograms (EEGs) of patients with mild cognitive impairment (MCI).
  Spectrum analysis was performed on the EEGs of MCI patients and healthy individuals,
  and the power spectrum values of the two groups were calculated and compared. Based
  on the power spectrum values of the theta and alpha bands, models for discriminating
  MCI patients were created using a support vector machine (SVM), and their accuracies
  were compared. In the spectrum analysis, the theta band showed significantly higher
  power spectrum values in the MCI group. In the comparison of the accuracies of SVM
  models, the model using the theta band showed higher values in parameters, such
  as accuracy rate. These results suggested that the power spectrum values of the
  theta band are useful for discriminating MCI patients.
