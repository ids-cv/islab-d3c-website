abstract: 'SUMMARY Image captioning models are known to perpetuate and amplify harmful
  societal bias in the training set. In this work, we aim to mitigate such gender
  bias in image captioning models. While prior work has addressed this problem by
  forcing models to focus on people to reduce gender misclassification, it conversely
  generates gender-stereotypical words at the expense of predicting the correct gender.
  From this observation, we hypothesize that there are two types of gender bias affecting
  image captioning models: 1) bias that exploits context to predict gender, and 2)
  bias in the probability of generating certain (often stereotypical) words because
  of gender. To mitigate both types of gender biases, we propose a framework, called
  LIBRA, that learns from synthetically biased samples to decrease both types of biases,
  correcting gender misclassification and changing gender-stereotypical words to more
  neutral ones.'
