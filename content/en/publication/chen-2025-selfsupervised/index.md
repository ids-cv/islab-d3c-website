abstract: Lattice light-sheet microscopy illuminates specimens with a thin light sheet
  to reduce image degradation caused by light scattering, unlike conventional uniform
  illumination. However, this approach is still unable to fully eliminate the blur
  introduced by the optical system. To address this problem, non-blind deconvolution
  techniques, such as Richardson-Lucy, are commonly used but they often introduce
  noise artifacts while deep supervised learning approaches require costly ground
  truth data. In this paper, we propose a self-supervised learning framework that
  explicitly integrates the imaging model with 3D neural network for reconstructing
  high-quality images without ground truth data by leveraging the optics of the microscopy
  and its point spread function. Extensive experiments demonstrate our superior performance
  over baseline algorithm both quantitatively and qualitatively.
