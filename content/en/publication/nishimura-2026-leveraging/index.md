abstract: Deep learning-based nuclei analysis in histopathology relies heavily on
  large-scale, expert-curated datasets that are costly and difficult to scale across
  organs. To address this, we propose a scalable framework leveraging cell-level spatial
  transcriptomics (ST) as an alternative supervision source for nuclei classification.
  Cell-level ST provides both gene expression profiles and nucleus masks, enabling
  automatic generation of image-label pairs without manual annotation. We convert
  gene expression into cell-type labels and introduce an image-oriented classification
  strategy to effectively transfer these identities to histopathology images. Evaluated
  on unseen tissues, our framework achieves comparable segmentation and classification
  performance compared with conventional supervised approaches, despite using fewer
  manually annotated datasets.
